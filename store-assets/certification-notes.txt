YES — testing this extension requires an account on the 智慧团建 platform (zhtj.youth.cn), but we cannot provide test credentials. This is an independent third-party tool, not affiliated with the platform.

Reason: accounts are issued through real CYL organizations and bound to real-name identity and real member records. Sharing an account would violate the platform's terms and China's Personal Information Protection Law, and would expose real members' names, phone numbers and password-reset codes. The platform has no sandbox, demo, or read-only test environment.

What can be verified without signing in:
1. Install the submitted ZIP. The extension requests only the storage permission and the zhtj.youth.cn host permission.
2. Open https://zhtj.youth.cn. The assistant panel appears on the right side of the page, below the top bar; the six tabs and the collapse/expand control can be checked.
3. In DevTools > Network, confirm that requests go only to zhtj.youth.cn. There is no remote code, no eval, no third-party endpoint, and no update checker. No data is sent to the developer.
4. The package contains only manifest.json, content.js, _locales/zh_CN/messages.json and four icons.
